# Consumer Agent scope

## In scope

- One consumer, one agent, one coffee machine, one merchant.
- One happy-path purchase and the refusal scenarios listed in the root `README.md`.
- Mandate creation, confirmation and use.

## Out of scope

- Real payments, real accounts or production endpoints.
- Multiple merchants or price comparison across merchants.
- Returns, refunds and disputes.
- Mandate management UI beyond what the demo needs.

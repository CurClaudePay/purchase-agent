# Mock data

Shared fictional data used by every team. Everything here is made up: no real
people, addresses, IBANs, iDEAL data or devices. Change it only in agreement
with the teams that use it.

- `coffee-machine.json`: the office coffee machine, its bean and milk levels
  and its maintenance schedule
- `products.json`: the merchant's catalogue of beans, milk and maintenance
- `accounts.json`: the consumer, the merchant and their sandbox wallets
